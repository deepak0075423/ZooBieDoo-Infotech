Overview

What you get from this practice

Short, sharply scoped assignments answering a specific question, delivered as a written report you can act on without us. The people named in the proposal do the work.

  • A written report, not a workshop you have to reconstruct
  • Findings ranked by business impact with costed remediation
  • Options presented with the case against each one included
  • Clear separation between advice and any delivery that follows
  • No obligation to use us for the work we recommend

Typical engagement

Starts with: a short, fixed-scope discovery

First release: in production early, not at the end

Team: named in the proposal

Exit: documented and rehearsed from day one

Capabilities

What this practice covers

Technology strategy

Target architecture, capability roadmap and investment sequencing aligned to business objectives and budget cycles.

Architecture review

Independent assessment of scalability, security, cost and maintainability, with prioritised findings and effort estimates.

Technical due diligence

Assessment of codebase, team, architecture and technical debt for investors, acquirers and boards.

Delivery recovery

Diagnosis and stabilisation of programmes that have lost schedule, scope or confidence.

Build-vs-buy analysis

Structured evaluation with weighted criteria, reference checks and total cost modelled over several years.

Interim technical leadership

Fractional CTO and lead architect support while you recruit permanently.

How we deliver

From first conversation to a running system

Step 01

Scope & access

A clear question, a fixed timeframe and the access needed to answer it properly — code, systems, people and numbers.

Step 02

Evidence gathering

Interviews, code and infrastructure review, and analysis of the metrics that already exist.

Step 03

Findings & options

Written findings with severity, options with trade-offs, and a recommendation we are prepared to defend.

Step 04

Support the decision

Presentation to your leadership team, then optional support through execution.
