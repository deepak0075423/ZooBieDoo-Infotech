Overview

What you get from this practice

Most of our QA work is on systems built by other people, including live estates under active change. We start with an assessment of current coverage and risk before proposing a programme.

  • Regression runs finishing inside the pipeline, not overnight
  • Flaky tests treated as defects, with an owner and a deadline
  • Performance budgets enforced per release rather than per crisis
  • Defect escape rate reported to the business every sprint
  • A test suite your developers extend without being asked

Typical engagement

Starts with: a short, fixed-scope discovery

First release: in production early, not at the end

Team: named in the proposal

Exit: documented and rehearsed from day one

Capabilities

What this practice covers

Test strategy & advisory

Risk-based test planning, environment strategy, tooling selection and quality metrics that inform decisions.

Test automation

Maintainable UI, API and integration suites in the pipeline, designed to survive refactors instead of being deleted.

Performance engineering

Load, stress, soak and spike testing with bottleneck analysis down to query and thread level.

Security testing

OWASP-aligned assessment, dependency and container scanning, and remediation guidance ranked by severity.

Accessibility testing

WCAG 2.2 AA validation combining automated coverage with manual assistive-technology testing.

Managed QA teams

Dedicated quality specialists embedded in your delivery squads under your release process.

How we deliver

From first conversation to a running system

Step 01

Quality assessment

Current coverage, defect trends, escaped-defect analysis and where the pipeline lets problems through.

Step 02

Design the pyramid

The right test at the right level, with explicit decisions about what will not be automated and why.

Step 03

Build & integrate

Frameworks built into CI with parallel execution, flake quarantine and reporting the whole team reads.

Step 04

Sustain

Suite health monitoring, a maintenance budget and periodic strategy review as the product changes.
