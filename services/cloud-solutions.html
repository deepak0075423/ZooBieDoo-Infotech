Overview

What you get from this practice

We do the assessment and landing-zone work before anything moves, migrate in rehearsed waves with a defined rollback, and hand over an operating model your own engineers can run without us.

  • Every environment reproducible from code, including production
  • Cost attributed to teams and features and reviewed monthly
  • Recovery objectives demonstrated by rehearsal, not asserted
  • Security guardrails enforced by policy rather than convention
  • An operating runbook your engineers own by the end

Typical engagement

Starts with: a short, fixed-scope discovery

First release: in production early, not at the end

Team: named in the proposal

Exit: documented and rehearsed from day one

Capabilities

What this practice covers

Cloud migration

Assessment, wave planning and execution across rehost, replatform and refactor paths, with rollback defined for every wave.

Cloud-native architecture

Containers, managed services and serverless designed around your actual traffic profile and failure tolerance.

Landing zones & governance

Account structure, network design, identity, tagging and policy guardrails codified from day one.

Cost optimisation

Spend attribution, rightsizing, commitment planning and unit-economics reporting the finance team can use.

Resilience engineering

Multi-AZ and multi-region design, with backup and restore rehearsed rather than assumed.

Managed cloud operations

Monitoring, patching, incident response and capacity planning under an agreed service level.

How we deliver

From first conversation to a running system

Step 01

Assess

Discovery of the estate: dependencies, licensing, data gravity, compliance constraints and a migration scorecard per workload.

Step 02

Design the landing zone

Accounts, networking, identity, logging and policy as code, established before the first workload moves.

Step 03

Migrate in waves

Low-risk workloads first to prove the runbook, then progressively harder ones with measured cutover windows.

Step 04

Optimise & operate

Post-migration tuning, cost review cycles and an operating model your team owns.
