Overview

What you get from this practice

We start by measuring how your delivery actually performs today and finding the real constraint — which is rarely the deployment step itself. The objective is always that your team owns the improvement after we leave.

  • Deployment lead time measured in minutes, not release windows
  • Rollback that has been rehearsed rather than theorised
  • Alert volume reduced to what a human can actually act on
  • Environment drift eliminated by code review and policy
  • On-call rotations your engineers are willing to join

Typical engagement

Starts with: a short, fixed-scope discovery

First release: in production early, not at the end

Team: named in the proposal

Exit: documented and rehearsed from day one

Capabilities

What this practice covers

CI/CD engineering

Build, test, security-scan and deploy pipelines with quality gates developers trust enough to keep.

Infrastructure as code

Every environment reproducible, reviewable and destroyable — including the one that matters.

Platform engineering

Internal developer platforms and golden paths that make the secure, observable option the easy one.

Observability

Metrics, logs, traces and service-level objectives wired to alerts that correspond to user pain rather than machine noise.

DevSecOps

Secrets management, dependency and container scanning, policy as code and supply-chain integrity in the pipeline.

Site reliability engineering

Error budgets, incident command, blameless post-mortems and capacity planning as an ongoing practice.

How we deliver

From first conversation to a running system

Step 01

Delivery assessment

We measure delivery performance as it stands, then find the constraint — usually not where the team assumes it is.

Step 02

Automate the critical path

Pipeline, environments and tests rebuilt around that constraint, with developer feedback time as the primary target.

Step 03

Instrument

Objectives defined with product owners, dashboards built for on-call reality, alerts pruned to the actionable.

Step 04

Transfer the practice

Pairing, documentation and on-call rotation design so the improvement outlives the engagement.
