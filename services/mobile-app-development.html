Overview

What you get from this practice

Our mobile practice is 31 people across native and cross-platform engineering, working to a single release standard whichever stack an engagement uses. We take products from first concept through store launch, and we stay on for the OS upgrades and store policy changes that arrive afterwards.

  • A store-ready build in the first eight weeks, not the last
  • Crash-free sessions above 99.5% held as a release gate
  • Cold-start budgets and frame-rate targets agreed before build
  • Accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA on both platforms
  • Full source, pipelines and store ownership transferred to you

Typical engagement

Discovery: 2–4 weeks, fixed price

Team: named in the proposal, contracted by seniority

Capabilities

What this practice covers

Native iOS & Android

Swift and Kotlin engineering for products where camera, offline, background processing or platform integration carry the experience.

Cross-platform delivery

React Native and Flutter builds with a shared design system, so two platforms stay in step without doubling the roadmap.

App modernisation

Rescue and re-architecture of ageing codebases: dependency debt, store compliance, crash rates and release cadence.

Backend & API layer

The services, sync engine and event pipeline behind the app, built for intermittent connectivity and constrained devices.

Release engineering

Automated build, signing, phased rollout and rollback across App Store Connect and Google Play.

Growth instrumentation

Analytics, funnels, remote config and experimentation wired in from the first build rather than retrofitted.

How we deliver

The path from first conversation to running system

Step 01

Product definition

Two to three weeks of discovery: user research, journey mapping, technical constraints and a release plan tied to business milestones.

Step 02

Architecture & design system

Navigation model, state management, offline strategy and a component library documented before feature work starts.

Step 03

Build in two-week increments

Working builds distributed to your team every sprint through TestFlight and internal tracks — never a six-month blind spot.

Step 04

Launch & operate

Staged rollout, store optimisation, crash triage and a support model that continues past go-live.
