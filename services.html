Engagement models

Four ways to work with us

The commercial shape should follow the uncertainty in the work.

Discovery engagement

Short and fixed scope

Research, technical assessment and a costed roadmap. The output is yours whether or not you continue with us, and it occasionally concludes that you should do something other than the build you asked about.

Project delivery

Costed increments, stoppable

A defined outcome delivered by a cross-discipline team under a named delivery lead, re-forecast each increment against actuals with a stop point at every boundary.

Dedicated engineering pod

Rolling monthly

A cross-functional team embedded in your organisation under your product direction. The same people month after month, with composition and seniority written into the contract.

Managed service & support

Retained, cancellable on notice

Ongoing operation, maintenance and enhancement of platforms we built or inherited, with defined response targets and a monthly allocation for changes.

Not sure which fits?

Most engagements start with discovery regardless of where they end up. It is the cheapest way to find out whether we are the right firm — and whether the work is worth doing at all.

Delivery method

The same four phases in every practice

Step 01

Discovery

Fixed-scope assessment producing a costed roadmap, architecture direction and risk register you own outright.

Step 02

Architecture

Decisions recorded with alternatives and consequences, reviewed across disciplines before build.

Step 03

Increments

Working software in production early and every fortnight after, behind feature flags.

Step 04

Handover

Documentation, pairing and phased transfer, rehearsed before the exit date.
