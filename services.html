Engagement models

Four ways to work with us

The commercial shape should follow the uncertainty in the work. Here is how we structure each.

Discovery engagement

2–4 weeks, fixed price

Research, technical assessment and a costed roadmap. The output is yours whether or not you continue with us — and roughly one in six discoveries ends with us recommending you do something other than the build you asked for.

Project delivery

Costed increments, stoppable

A defined outcome delivered by a cross-practice team under a named delivery lead. Re-forecast each increment against actuals, with a stop point at every boundary.

Dedicated engineering pods

Rolling, 3-month minimum

A cross-functional team embedded in your organisation under your product direction. Same people, month after month — team composition and seniority written into the contract.

Managed service

Retained, 30 days' notice

Ongoing operation, support and enhancement of platforms we built or inherited, with defined response and resolution targets and a monthly change allocation.

Not sure which fits?

Most engagements start with discovery regardless of where they end up. It is the cheapest way to find out whether we are the right firm — and whether the work is worth doing at all.
