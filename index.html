How we work

Four commitments that shape every engagement

Consultancies are easy to hire and harder to trust. These are the things we hold to, and what each one costs us.

  1. Understand before proposing

    We spend the first phase with the people who do the work, not only the people who commission the system. It is common for discovery to change the scope that was originally requested.

  2. Ship in increments that stand alone

    Value lands in production early and at every increment after, so you are never a long way into a programme with nothing running.

  3. Make decisions reviewable

    Architecture decision records, costed options and written trade-offs. Anyone joining later can see why the system is the way it is.

  4. Plan the handover from day one

    Documentation, pairing and phased responsibility transfer are in the plan from the start, not improvised at the end.

Our delivery model

The same four phases, whatever the practice

Predictability comes from method rather than from optimism in the plan.

Step 01

Discovery

A short, fixed-scope phase producing a costed roadmap, an architecture direction and a risk register you own outright.

Step 02

Architecture

Decisions recorded with alternatives and consequences, reviewed before build starts.

Step 03

Increments

Working software into production early and every fortnight after, behind feature flags.

Step 04

Handover

Documentation, pairing and phased transfer, planned from day one rather than improvised at the end.
