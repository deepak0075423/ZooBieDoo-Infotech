How we work

Consultancies are easy to hire and hard to trust. We try to earn the second part.

Four commitments that shape every engagement, written into contracts rather than left on a values page.

  1. Understand before proposing

    We spend the first weeks with the people who do the work, not only the people who commission it. Roughly a third of our discovery phases materially change the scope that was requested.

  2. Ship in increments that stand alone

    Value lands in production within the first quarter and every increment after. You are never a year into a programme with nothing running.

  3. Make decisions reviewable

    Architecture decision records, costed options and written trade-offs. Anyone joining in month nine can see why the system is the way it is.

  4. Plan the handover from day one

    Documentation, pairing and phased responsibility transfer are in the plan from the start, not improvised at the end.

Our delivery model

The same four phases, whatever the practice

Predictability comes from a repeatable method, not from optimism in the plan.

Step 01

Discovery

Two to four weeks, fixed price. Research, technical assessment and a costed roadmap you own whether or not you continue with us.

Step 02

Architecture

Decisions recorded with alternatives and consequences. The plan is reviewable by anyone who joins later.

Step 03

Increments

Working software in production from the first quarter, every fortnight after. Stoppable at any boundary.

Step 04

Handover

Documentation, pairing and phased transfer. Every engagement has a defined exit from day one.
