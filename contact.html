What happens next

No qualification call, no discovery deck

Your enquiry goes to a principal, not to a sales development queue. Here is the sequence.

1. We reply within one working day

Usually the same day. If we are not the right firm for the problem, we will say so and suggest who might be.

2. A 60-minute conversation

With two principals — one from the relevant practice, one from delivery. Bring the problem, not a specification.

3. A written point of view

Within a week: what we think the problem is, the options as we see them, and an indicative shape and cost.

4. Discovery, if it makes sense

Two to four weeks, fixed price, and you own everything it produces regardless of what you do next.
