How we work

Why here

Client work, with seniors alongside you rather than above you

Consultancy has a reputation, some of it earned: unclear progression, being sold as a profile rather than hired as a person, and years spent adjacent to the work rather than doing it. We try to run against each of those.

People here are on client-facing delivery early, with senior engineers and designers working alongside them rather than reviewing from a distance. Progression expectations are written down per discipline and reviewed on a regular cycle, so what “the next level” means is not a matter of opinion.

  • Real project work from early on, not shadowing
  • Written progression expectations for every discipline
  • Code review, CI and time budgeted for maintenance as standard
  • Support for certification in the platforms we work with
  • Sustained overtime treated as a planning failure, not a badge

Hiring process

Four steps, and an answer either way

We tell you where you stand at each stage rather than leaving applications open indefinitely.

Step 01

Application review

A person reads every application and replies either way. We tell you where you stand rather than leaving it open.

Step 02

Discipline conversation

A conversation with the lead of the practice you would join — their questions and yours, no whiteboard puzzles.

Step 03

Practical exercise

A realistic problem, discussed afterwards. We are interested in how you reason, not whether you have memorised an algorithm.

Step 04

Team & offer

Meet people you would work with day to day, then an offer with the role, level and progression expectations set out clearly.
