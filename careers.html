Why here

A consultancy that behaves like a product company internally

Consultancy has a reputation, largely earned: utilisation targets, unclear progression, and being sold as a profile rather than hired as a person. We have built deliberately against each of those.

Our utilisation target is 72 percent, not 95, and the remainder is real — learning, internal work and recovery between engagements. Progression frameworks are published for every discipline, with twice-yearly reviews against them. And nobody is put in front of a client at a level they are not working at.

  • Salary bands published internally, reviewed against market annually
  • Utilisation target of 72% — the rest is genuinely yours
  • Every engineer spends time on delivery, not only on estimates
  • Principals still write code, design and review
  • No unpaid overtime culture; sustained overrun is treated as a planning failure

Hiring process

Four steps, three weeks, one answer either way

We pay for the technical exercise because your time has value whether or not we make an offer.

Step 01

Application review

A person reads it, and everyone gets an answer within five working days. No silent rejections.

Step 02

Practice conversation

45 minutes with the principal who leads the practice you would join. Their questions, your questions, no whiteboard.

Step 03

Technical session

A two-hour paid exercise on a realistic problem, discussed afterwards. No algorithm puzzles and no take-home marathon.

Step 04

Team and offer

Meet two people you would work with day to day, then an offer with the salary band and progression framework attached.
