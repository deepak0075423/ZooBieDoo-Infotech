How we deliver

Who we are

Built around delivery, not around a sales process

ZooBieDoo Infotech is an engineering and design consultancy. We build web platforms and Drupal sites, mobile applications, custom software, cloud infrastructure and data systems — and then we stay involved for the maintenance, security updates and changes that follow, because that is where most of a system's life is spent.

We work the way we do because of a pattern most people in this industry recognise: capable teams delivering the wrong thing competently, because nobody was willing to say early enough that the plan was wrong. Our process is built to surface that early, in writing, while it is still cheap to change.

Practically, that means a short discovery phase before anything is committed, working software in production early rather than at the end, decisions recorded so they can be reviewed, and a handover plan from day one.

What we hold to

Four commitments, and what each one costs

Values only mean something when they cost you something. Here is what these cost us.

Say the difficult thing early

Bad news does not improve with age. Problems reach the people who can act on them quickly, in writing, with options attached.

Recommend against ourselves when it is right

Sometimes the correct answer is to buy rather than build, to keep an existing supplier, or not to proceed. We would rather say so than win the work.

Write it down

Architecture decisions, trade-offs, incident reviews and handover plans exist as documents. Knowledge that lives only in someone's head is a risk we price out deliberately.

Leave clients independent

Every engagement has a defined exit. A client taking work in-house because we prepared them to is the engagement working, not failing.

How we handle your information

Practices we can be held to

Security and confidentiality commitments that form part of the engagement agreement rather than a marketing page.

  • Non-disclosure and data processing agreements as standard
  • Least-privilege access, reviewed at the start and end of engagements
  • Anonymised or synthetic data in non-production environments wherever possible
  • Client data processed only in the locations agreed with you
  • Accessibility to WCAG 2.2 AA as a default delivery standard

We complete supplier security questionnaires as part of onboarding and are happy to work to your organisation's own policies and review processes.
