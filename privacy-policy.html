Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do about it. Written to be read rather than to be defensible.

Summary

We collect what you send us through forms and a small amount of technical data about how the site is used. We use it to reply to you, to run engagements and to improve the site. We do not sell it, we do not use advertising trackers, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Zoobiedoo Infotech ("Zoobiedoo", "we", "us") is an engineering and design consultancy with its registered office at Tower B, 7th Floor, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India, and operating offices in London, Singapore and Austin.

This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and in the course of our business relationships, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. For the purposes of the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation, Zoobiedoo Infotech is the data controller for the processing described here.

Questions about this policy, or about data we hold, should go to our data protection contact at privacy@zoobiedoo.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data.

Information you give us

  • Enquiry details — name, work email address, telephone number, company, job title, and the content of your message when you complete a form or email us.
  • Application details — name, contact details, work history, portfolio or repository links, right-to-work information and anything else you choose to include when you apply for a role.
  • Subscription details — the email address you provide for our quarterly briefing.
  • Engagement records — contact details and correspondence generated in the course of delivering work for a client organisation.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, and the pages you visit on this site.
  • Usage data — how you arrived at the site, how long you spend on pages, and which links you follow.

We do not collect special category data through this website, and we ask that you do not include it in enquiry or application forms.

3. How we use your information

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and to prepare for and hold conversations about potential work.
  • To assess applications for employment and to communicate with candidates.
  • To deliver, administer and support engagements for client organisations.
  • To send our quarterly briefing where you have asked to receive it.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, accounting and reporting obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — responding to business enquiries, managing client relationships, securing our systems and understanding how our website is used. We have assessed that this processing does not override your interests or rights.
  • Contract — where processing is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you or the organisation you represent.
  • Consent — for the quarterly briefing and for non-essential cookies. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data to comply with applicable law.

5. Who we share information with

We share personal data only where it is necessary, and only with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under written instructions — including hosting, email, customer relationship management, applicant tracking and analytics providers. Each is bound by a data processing agreement.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and insurers, where required.
  • Authorities where disclosure is required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Group offices — our teams in India, the United Kingdom, Singapore and the United States, under a common set of internal controls.

We do not transfer client data outside the jurisdictions agreed in the relevant engagement contract.

6. International transfers

Personal data may be processed in India, the United Kingdom, Singapore and the United States. Where data is transferred out of the UK or European Economic Area, we rely on adequacy decisions where they exist, and otherwise on Standard Contractual Clauses together with supplementary technical and organisational measures.

You may request a copy of the transfer mechanism applicable to your data by writing to privacy@zoobiedoo.com.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it:

CategoryRetention period
Website enquiries24 months from last contact
Unsuccessful job applications6 months, or longer with your consent
Briefing subscriptionsUntil you unsubscribe
Client engagement records7 years after the engagement ends
Website analytics14 months

At the end of the applicable period, data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

8. Your rights

Subject to the law applicable to you, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Have data erased where we no longer have grounds to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to processing carried out under legitimate interests.
  • Receive data in a portable format where processing is based on consent or contract.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis for processing.

To exercise any of these rights, write to privacy@zoobiedoo.com. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local supervisory authority — in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office.

9. Cookies

This website uses a deliberately small number of cookies.

  • Strictly necessary cookies keep the site secure and functioning. These cannot be switched off and do not require consent.
  • Analytics cookies help us understand which pages are useful. These are set only with your consent and the data is aggregated.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not embed third-party social media trackers.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

10. Security

We maintain information security controls aligned to ISO 27001, including access control on a least-privilege basis, encryption of data in transit and at rest, logging and monitoring, background checks for staff on regulated engagements, and annual third-party assessment.

No system is perfectly secure. Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where the risk is high, notify you directly.

11. Children

This website is directed at business audiences and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We review this policy at least annually and when our processing changes materially. The version in force is always published on this page with its effective date. Where a change materially affects how we use data you have already provided, we will tell you directly.

13. How to contact us

Write to the data protection contact at privacy@zoobiedoo.com, or by post to: Data Protection, Zoobiedoo Infotech, Tower B, 7th Floor, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India.

For general enquiries, see our contact page.

This policy is provided as part of a website build and should be reviewed by your own legal counsel before publication, so that it reflects your actual processing activities, retention practice and regulatory footprint.
