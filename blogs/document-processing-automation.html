Automating document processing without losing accuracy

Extracting structured data from real-world documents is achievable. The engineering is in confidence thresholds and review design.

ZooBieDoo Infotech Engineering Team 13 July 2026 · 7 min read

Key takeaways

  • Assess on genuinely messy documents, not clean templates.
  • Set confidence thresholds per field by cost of error.
  • The review workspace determines the system's throughput.
  • Validate against systems of record before writing anything.

Real documents are not clean templates

Commercial extraction tools perform well on consistent forms and poorly on the photographed, handwritten and scanned material that makes up most genuine intake — which is exactly the part consuming the time.

Assess against a stratified sample weighted towards the difficult end, not against the tidy examples.

Confidence thresholds per field

The cost of being wrong differs by field. A reference number read incorrectly is an inconvenience; a payment amount read incorrectly is a serious problem.

Set thresholds per field according to that cost. Anything below routes to human review; anything above is written automatically. This single decision determines both the automation rate and the risk profile.

Design the review workspace

  • Show the extracted value beside the region of the source it came from.
  • Allow correction in one action, without re-keying the rest.
  • Reject any extraction that cannot be traced to a source region.
  • Capture corrections as labelled data for future evaluation.

The review interface determines the throughput of the whole system, and it is routinely built last with the least attention.

Validate against systems of record

Never write extracted values straight into a business system. Validate against what you already know: does the reference exist, does the amount fall within the policy limit, does the date make sense.

Business-rule validation catches a substantial share of extraction errors regardless of model quality, and it is cheap to implement.

Measuring the business outcome

Extraction accuracy is an engineering measure. The business measure is what proportion of documents complete without human involvement, and how much handling time fell.

Track straight-through processing rate, time per document end to end, and the rework rate where an automated extraction later proved wrong. These are what justify the investment and what reveal whether thresholds are set sensibly.

A system with high accuracy and low straight-through rate usually has thresholds set too conservatively, which is a tuning decision rather than a model problem.

Frequently asked questions
